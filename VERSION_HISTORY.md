## Versioning policy
- Increment the project version for every approved change that alters project artefacts.
- Record the exact commit SHA for each version.
- Record changed files and checksums.
- Explicitly identify carried-forward files.
- Record inspections, automated tests, compilation and device tests separately.
- Keep unresolved requirements visible; do not silently convert proposals into fixed requirements.
